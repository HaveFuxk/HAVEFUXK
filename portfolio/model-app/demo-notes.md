# MODEL-APP — screenshot walkthrough

This 132-second silent English-captioned video presents captured web UI on a black canvas. It is a screenshot walkthrough, not a continuous screen recording or a native iOS/Android-device recording.

The real web captures remain intact and are scaled proportionally. Titles and captions sit outside the app image. The two **Synthetic demo** scenes show a separate teaching sample with simulated success and rollback; they do not depict requests or failures triggered in MODEL-APP.

No post or comment is claimed to have been submitted in this walkthrough. Likes are discussed as an implementation pattern; the video does not establish production reliability or measure performance.

MODEL-APP is available on the [web](https://poseshelf.com), [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6782850253), and through a direct [Android APK download](https://poseshelf.com/android). Android is not yet listed on Google Play.

My contribution includes requirements, user flows, AI-assisted implementation, validation, issue tracking, and release documentation. I am continuing to strengthen independent code reading, explanation, and modification.

Deliverables: `model-app-walkthrough.mp4` (1280×720, 30 fps, H.264, no audio) and `demo.srt` (English captions; also burned into the video).

The walkthrough contains 11 scenes of 12 seconds each. Application screens are supplied captures, not generated substitutes. The separate synthetic sample is explicitly labelled on screen.
